# LiteKnight v0.5.0-dev

**Version:** `0.5.0-dev`
**Tag:** `v0.5.0-dev`
**Status:** proper 360° aiming, weapon energy costs, Knight Dual Wield, three
actual dungeon rooms and readable recovered-asset HUD.

## Highlights

- Full 360° mouse aiming independent of WASD movement. The body flips on the
  vertical aim axis with a documented hysteresis band; weapon rotation,
  muzzle placement and projectile direction all follow the aim vector.
- Manual / Auto Aim setting persisted in save v2. Auto Aim ignores dead enemies,
  rejects wall-obstructed targets and releases the held pose when not firing.
- Recovered per-attack energy costs from serialized `consume` plus IL2CPP
  `RGWeapon.MakeConsume`/`MultiHand.CheckEnergyEnough`:
  `weapon_000=0`, `weapon_002=1`, `weapon_048=1`, `weapon_287=1`.
  Insufficient energy rejects the attack; passive `+1 / 2.0 s` regeneration is
  preserved.
- Knight c00 Dual Wield on `Q`: recovered `maxTime=5.0`, `cd=10.0`, no energy
  cost, `ui_skill01` HUD icon, off-hand weapon fire/render and cooldown mask.
- `Tab` switches weapons; `1`/`2` select slots.
- Actual recovered PatternRooms `r1_1`, `r1_2`, `r1_3` loaded simultaneously
  with unique obstacle/topology data. Fixed physical door graph plus a
  deterministic `--map-seed` assignment using the reconstructed RNG.
- Walking through an open door changes rooms at the matching entrance. Enemy
  snapshots and room-clear state persist across visits.
- HUD cleanup: upper-left status panel, upper-right pause/coin strip,
  bottom-left weapon strip, larger bottom-left Knight power button, compact
  bottom-centre pickup card with damage/energy/cycle stats.
- `Esc` pause hierarchy fixed (settings backs out to pause; simulation frozen).

## Evidence boundary

`CONFIRMED`: c00 SkillInfo values, weapon `consume` energy path, 108 actual
PatternRoom prefabs, unique integrated room data, 28 px font metrics, passive
energy regeneration.

`HYPOTHESIS`: body-flip threshold/hysteresis, auto-aim target priority, native
three-room topology and seed assignment, cooldown start phase, single-weapon
off-hand duplication.

`UNKNOWN`: reference `MapManagerLevel.CreateMap/CreateRoom`, exact auto-aim
selection function, off-hand animation/muzzle chain, full 108-room runtime
catalogue and wide-catalogue weapon costs.

## Testing

- 139 doctest cases / 5,167 assertions.
- Three CTest entries: unit tests, asset metadata audit, HeroRoom audit.
- RelWithDebInfo and Debug ASan/UBSan builds; Vulkan validation clean.
- Devroom smoke, Living Room smoke, physical room transition, room-state
  persistence, save/load across rooms, aim-mode persistence and pause/resume
  scenarios executed.

## Known limitations

- 3 of 108 discovered PatternRooms are integrated.
- Reference procedural generation is not reconstructed.
- EnemyAI04 scouting/pathfinding, forge/plant economy and most catalogue content
  remain incomplete.
- Panel/ribbon/menu-background art remains native approximation; exact frame
  mapping is UNKNOWN.

Reference-derived pixels/font/title assets remain `LOCAL_ONLY`,
`REFERENCE_DERIVED`, `NOT_FOR_REDISTRIBUTION`. Native source is MIT.
LiteKnight is unofficial and unaffiliated with ChillyRoom.
