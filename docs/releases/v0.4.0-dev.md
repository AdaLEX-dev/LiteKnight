# LiteKnight v0.4.0-dev

**Version:** `0.4.0-dev`
**Tag:** `v0.4.0-dev`
**Status:** readable recovered-asset UI, bordered fullscreen, Hub cleanup, map inspection and confirmed energy regeneration

## Highlights

- Recovered `pixel_bold` text rasterized at 28 px with corrected line height,
  dark shadows/outline and readable menus/HUD.
- Recovered HUD heart/shield/crystal/pause/coin icons.
- Recovered button frames and pause/home/resume/settings/plus icons.
- Recovered Soul Knight title logo and main-menu button styling.
- Bordered fullscreen with integer-scale preference and 16:9 letterboxing.
- Living Room mask/void cleanup and a floor-tile fill for unrecoverable cells.
- Map/room state inspection, entrance teleport, and room-clear door flow.
- Passive energy regeneration confirmed by IL2CPP disassembly: +1 every 2.0 s.
- UI asset audit and additional local UI/font tests.

## Known limitations

- Only two actual scene rooms are loaded: Living Room and `r1_1`.
- Procedural dungeon generation and full room graph remain UNKNOWN.
- Additional enemies/weapons, forge/plant economy and character abilities remain
  partial or placeholder foundations.
- Some large UI panel frames and main-menu background remain native
  approximations; exact mappings are documented in `analysis/assets/ui_audit.*`.
- Mobile joystick/touch UI and the social tab are intentionally absent.

Reference-derived UI/font/title pixels remain `LOCAL_ONLY`,
`REFERENCE_DERIVED`, `NOT_FOR_REDISTRIBUTION`. Native source is MIT. Soul Knight
belongs to its respective rights holders; LiteKnight is unofficial and
unaffiliated.
