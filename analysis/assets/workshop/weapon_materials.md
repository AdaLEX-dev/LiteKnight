## Notes

`WeaponMaterial` uses its serialized `materialFactor`, `materials`, and `extraMaterials` arrays. `CustomWeaponMaterial` uses its Odin `Dictionary<GameObject,int>` and `isAddtional`. The default fallback resolves `DefaultWeaponMaterial.defaultMaterial` PPtrs through `SerializationData.ReferencedUnityObjects` and the external material asset files (`material_iron`, `material_gear`, `material_battery`, `material_wood`, `material_cell`, `material_gem`).
